Career Opportunities in Clean Energy

Join our mission to strengthen renewable technologies, manage smart grids, and deliver public engagement initiatives for clean energy solutions.

Renewable Energy Systems Engineer

Job Reference Number: RE109

Short Description: We are looking for a skilled engineer to build, optimize, and evaluate commercial solar and wind infrastructure platforms while maintaining clean system data workflows.

Role Position Details

  • Salary: $95,000 - $112,000 AUD per annum
  • Reporting Line: Reports directly to the Principal Projects Director

Key Responsibilities

  1. Design and test operational models for commercial clean energy integration.
  2. Collaborate with web developers to present real-time performance analytics on community portals.
  3. Review public safety protocols and system security updates systematically.

Position Requirements

  • Essential: Bachelor’s degree in Engineering or Renewable Technologies, and 2+ years of grid-modeling experience.
  • Preferable: Familiarity with smart-grid monitoring tools and collaborative project management spaces like Jira.

Digital Engagement Content Officer

Job Reference Number: CO482

Short Description: Drive public engagement initiatives by maintaining our suite of client-facing platforms, writing clean environmental impact copy, and managing accessible campaign data structures.

Role Position Details

  • Salary: $72,000 - $81,000 AUD per annum
  • Reporting Line: Reports directly to the Communications Strategy Manager

Key Responsibilities

  • Author accessible web copy detailing our latest project landmarks and carbon-neutral targets.
  • Liaise directly with front-end technical groups to maintain structural page elements without broken links.
  • Coordinate public community webinars focused on neighborhood green solutions.

Position Requirements

  • Essential: Proven baseline background in corporate communications, clear writing mechanics, and fundamental awareness of web layout structures.
  • Preferable: Prior work experience inside a sustainable energy environment, or working knowledge of basic WCAG web readability strategies.
